◆ timestamp

uint64_t libe3::Pdu::timestamp {0}

Producer timestamp, in nanoseconds since the Unix epoch.

Stamped from the realtime clock (see now_realtime_ns) when the PDU is constructed, so a caller gets one for free. Zero means "no producer reference": every encoding transmits it as an absent field and decodes an absent field back to zero, so the clock read is opt-out and a PDU without a timestamp is a normal message rather than a malformed one.

Warning
Realtime, not monotonic. Never subtract it from a monotonic reading.
