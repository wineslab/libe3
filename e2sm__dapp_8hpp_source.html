1/**
2 * @file e2sm_dapp.hpp
3 * @brief E2SM-DAPP information elements and their APER codec (C++ API).
4 *
5 * E2SM-DAPP is the E2 service model (RAN function id 255, OID
6 * 1.3.6.1.4.1.53148.1.1.255.3) that carries dApp traffic between an E2 node and
7 * an xApp. Its grammar ships with libe3 as
8 * `messages/asn1/e2sm_dapp/V1/e2sm_dapp-1.0.0.asn`, and the specification is in
9 * `docs/e2sm-dapp/`.
10 *
11 * The type and field names in this header deliberately mirror OCUDU's
12 * `asn1::e2sm_dapp` (`include/ocudu/asn1/e2sm/e2sm_dapp.h`), so that code
13 * building or reading a message looks the same in both stacks. The codec behind
14 * it is asn1c here and OCUDU's own ASN.1 runtime there; the two are checked
15 * against each other with golden vectors. Differences from the OCUDU interface:
16 * - only the eight top-level types (see below) have `pack` and `unpack`, and they
17 * work on `std::vector<uint8_t>` instead of `bit_ref`;
18 * - the ASN.1 helper templates (`printable_string`, `fixed_octstring`, ...) are
19 * thin wrappers over `std::string` and `std::vector`;
20 * - there is no `to_json`;
21 * - `operator==` is provided on every type.
22 *
23 * Top-level types, each the content of an E2AP OCTET STRING:
24 * `e2sm_dapp_event_trigger_s`, `e2sm_dapp_action_definition_s`,
25 * `e2sm_dapp_ind_hdr_s`, `e2sm_dapp_ind_msg_s`, `e2sm_dapp_ctrl_hdr_s`,
26 * `e2sm_dapp_ctrl_msg_s`, `e2sm_dapp_ctrl_outcome_s` and
27 * `e2sm_dapp_ran_function_definition_s`.
28 *
29 * Built only when LIBE3_ENABLE_E2SM_DAPP is on (it needs LIBE3_ENABLE_ASN1).
30 *
31 * SPDX-FileCopyrightText: Copyright (c) 2026 Northeastern University
32 * SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0
33 */

60/// Mirrors OCUDU's `enumerated<Opts, Ext>`: derives from the options struct and converts to its enum.

64public:

69 /// @param v value to hold

71 /// @return the held value
72 operator typename EnumType::options() const { return EnumType::value; }
