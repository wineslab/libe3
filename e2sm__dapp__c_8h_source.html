1/**
2 * @file e2sm_dapp_c.h
3 * @brief E2SM-DAPP information elements and their APER codec (C API).
4 *
5 * The C counterpart of @ref e2sm_dapp.hpp, for RAN stacks written in C (flexric,
6 * and any stack that links libe3 from C). The structs carry the same information
7 * as the C++ types, with one change: every OPTIONAL field has an explicit
8 * `has_*` flag, and 0 is an ordinary value, never a stand-in for "absent".
9 *
10 * Ownership:
11 * - Inputs to `libe3_e2sm_dapp_encode_*` belong to the caller and are only read.
12 * - `libe3_e2sm_dapp_encode_*` allocates `out->data`; release it with
13 * @ref libe3_e2sm_dapp_bytes_free.
14 * - `libe3_e2sm_dapp_decode_*` allocates every pointer inside `*out`; release the
15 * whole struct with the matching `libe3_e2sm_dapp_free_*`. On failure `*out` is
16 * zeroed and nothing needs freeing.
17 * - Every `free` function zeroes the struct, so freeing twice is harmless.
18 *
19 * Return codes are the values of @ref e3_error_t. They are 0 on success, and
20 * E3_INVALID_PARAM, E3_ENCODE_FAILED, E3_DECODE_FAILED or E3_SM_ERROR_MEMORY on failure.
21 *
22 * Built only when LIBE3_ENABLE_E2SM_DAPP is on (it needs LIBE3_ENABLE_ASN1).
23 *
24 * SPDX-FileCopyrightText: Copyright (c) 2026 Northeastern University
25 * SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0
26 */
